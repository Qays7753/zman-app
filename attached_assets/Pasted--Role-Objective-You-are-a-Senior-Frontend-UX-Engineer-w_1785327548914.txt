# The Problem
The current `dashboard.tsx` is over 1000 lines long and acts as a "wall of cards" (10+ cards). It shows 5 different profit numbers, duplicates inventory/asset info, and overwhelms the mobile user. The owner cannot answer "Am I okay today?" in 3 seconds.

# The Proposed UX Logic (The "Dashboard Pyramid")
We want to restructure the dashboard into 3 layers. Since we already created dedicated `/inventory` and `/assets` screens in Round 1, the dashboard no longer needs to list everything.

1. **Layer 1 (Always Visible - The Quick Health Check):** 
   - "Cash Now" (Cash + Bank balance).
   - "This Month's Status" (Sales - Expenses = Operational Profit, clearly color-coded).
   - "Smart Alerts" (e.g., 2 delayed orders, low stock items).
2. **Layer 2 (Collapsible - "Show Financial Details"):**
   - A button that reveals deeper cards: Inventory Total Value (with a link to `/inventory`), Assets Net Value (with a link to `/assets`), Top Expenses this month.
3. **Layer 3 (To be removed/moved):**
   - Remove the long descriptive "Financial Advisor" text.
   - Remove duplicated profit numbers (keep only Operational Profit in Layer 1, move the rest to the Reports screen).
   - Remove the detailed tracked inventory list (since it has its own page now).
