# The Core Problem (The Owner's Pain Point)
Historically, the system was simple. If the owner bought inventory (e.g., concrete pots), they recorded it as an "Expense". The system deducted it from cash AND reduced the displayed profit. This was demotivating and mathematically wrong, as the money wasn't lost; it was converted from cash to inventory.

We upgraded the backend to track inventory and calculate COGS (Cost of Goods Sold) and Operational Profit. The backend is now correct. 

HOWEVER, the frontend UI (especially the Dashboard) still uses misleading terminology and grouping. When the owner sees "Expenses" and "Purchases" grouped together, or sees their profit drop after buying inventory, they still feel like they lost money, even though the backend calculates Operational Profit correctly. The UI doesn't visually explain: "Buying inventory is a cash conversion, not a profit loss."
